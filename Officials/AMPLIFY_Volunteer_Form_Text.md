# AMPLIFY Volunteer Programme - Form Introduction

Thank you for your interest in joining the AMPLIFY volunteer community. This form is the first step to become part of the team that supports our campaign, helps coordinate activities, and participates in the WhatsApp communication groups used by organisers and volunteers.

## About this volunteer programme

- This is a voluntary, non-paid campaign. No direct financial compensation, salary, or employment contract is offered through this form.
- Volunteers are expected to behave respectfully in all group chats, meetings, calls, and activities.
- A participation certificate is available only after you complete the programme and fulfil the assigned tasks successfully.
- If you are under 18 years old, you must have guardian permission and provide a guardian contact.

## Why we collect your information

Your information is used to:

- add you to the correct WhatsApp coordination group,
- assign you to the best-suited volunteer area,
- support your onboarding and training,
- share important event updates and schedules,
- issue a certificate at the end of the programme if you meet the completion requirements.

We will only share your data with the volunteer coordination team and the people responsible for managing the campaign.

## How to use this form

Please answer honestly and fully. Choose the areas where you can contribute best, and tell us about your availability, skills, and experience.

### Suggested volunteer fields and questions

1. Personal details
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - WhatsApp phone number
   - Country / city
   - Age or date of birth
   - If under 18, guardian name and guardian contact details

2. Availability
   - Days and times you can support the campaign
   - Whether you can attend the mandatory onboarding call

3. Volunteer experience and skills
   - Have you volunteered online or in advocacy events before?
   - What languages do you speak?
   - Which tools do you feel comfortable using? (WhatsApp, email, Zoom, Google Drive, Canva, etc.)

4. Department preference
   Select the volunteer area that fits you best. You may choose more than one if the form allows it.
   - Communications & social media
   - On-ground operations & logistics
   - Outreach & registration
   - Digital support & design
   - Participant care & facilitation
   - Translation / interpretation
   - Content creation & reporting
   - Monitoring & evaluation

5. Motivation and commitment
   - Why do you want to volunteer for this campaign?
   - What strengths or experience will you bring?
   - Are you comfortable working respectfully in WhatsApp groups and following team instructions?

## Behaviour and conditions summary

Please confirm that you understand and agree with the following points:

- I consent to participate as a volunteer, not as an employee.
- I understand that there is no direct financial compensation.
- I accept that my certificate depends on real completion of the programme.
- I agree to follow the programme rules and work respectfully with everyone.

## Where to place age and free participation notes

Put a short summary in the form description so volunteers see it immediately. The full details and the official conditions should also be available as a link to the terms page.

Example summary text for the top of the form:

> This volunteer campaign is free to join and is not paid. If you are under 18, guardian permission is required. A certificate will be issued only after the volunteer programme is completed successfully.
